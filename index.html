OUR STORY

BASED IN INCHEON, KOREA

Built around the
journey ahead.

JHT Korea began with a simple idea: make it easier for people around the world to buy a vehicle from Korea with a real person guiding the process. Based in Incheon, we source quality used cars, explain the details and help them make their way to the right destination.

From the first conversation through export paperwork and shipment, our work is about clarity and care. We’re here for the car you choose—and the road that follows.
