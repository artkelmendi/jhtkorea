Finding a car

Can you find a vehicle that is not listed?

Yes. Send the make, model, year and features you need. JHT can search Korean auctions and dealer networks; the existing FAQ says this typically takes three to seven business days.

Which makes and vehicle types do you handle?

JHT's existing site names Hyundai, Kia, Genesis, SsangYong, Chevrolet and German makes sourced in Korea. Sedans, SUVs, vans, trucks and other body styles may be available. The cars on this site are a sample collection.

Can I review a car's condition before deciding?

Ask for available photos, inspection information and history for that exact car. Check the reference number and all details before requesting an invoice.

Buying & payment

How do I reserve a vehicle?

Send the vehicle reference number or VIN with your consignee details. JHT prepares a proforma invoice; its purchase guide says a selected car can be reserved for three business days.

How is payment arranged?

The purchase guide describes a bank wire transfer using the proforma invoice, followed by sending the bank receipt to the official JHT WhatsApp number or email. Confirm the current method and beneficiary details with the team before paying.

Is shipping included in the vehicle price?

Shipping can be quoted separately. Ask for a destination-specific total, including freight and applicable fees, before committing.

Shipping & arrival

What is the difference between RoRo and container shipping?

With RoRo, a drivable vehicle rolls onto the ship. Container shipping secures it in a steel container and may suit vehicles travelling with parts. Route, availability and price vary.

How long does shipping take?

Transit depends on destination, vessel and port conditions. The old FAQ estimates some routes at roughly three to six weeks. Ask the team for a current sailing and arrival estimate.

How can I follow the shipment?

After shipment, JHT says it shares a bill of lading number, vessel information and an estimated arrival date for carrier tracking.

Which documents will I need?

The purchase guide covers the draft and original bill of lading. Export and destination requirements vary by country; check with a local customs agent before buying.
