THE PURCHASE GUIDE

From choice
to arrival.

Six clear steps from selecting a vehicle in Korea to collecting it at your destination.

THE ROUTE / 06 STEPS

Keep the vehicle reference, consignee information and official invoice together as you go.

Explore vehicles ↗
01

Choose & request an invoice

Send the vehicle reference number or VIN with full consignee details. JHT issues a proforma invoice by email or WhatsApp. Its old guide says the car can be reserved for three business days.

02

Make the payment

Use the official invoice to arrange a bank wire transfer, then send the receipt to JHT for verification. Confirm payment instructions with the team before sending funds.

03

Book the shipment

After payment is confirmed, JHT arranges vessel space and sends a booking notice.

04

Review the documents

After departure, check the draft bill of lading carefully. Once confirmed, original documents are sent by international express.

05

Track the journey

Use the carrier and shipment numbers provided to follow the vehicle's progress and estimated arrival.

06

Clear customs & collect

Arrange customs clearance and final pickup at your destination. JHT recommends working with a local customs agent.

Before you buy

Used vehicles may have cosmetic wear and consumable parts that need replacement. Review the details of the exact car and ask about any concern before paying. Import rules and fees depend on your destination.
